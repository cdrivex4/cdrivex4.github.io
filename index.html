Background

Infrastructure at scale.
Engineering by curiosity.

I am the System Support Manager at the Seychelles Infrastructure Agency (SIA), where my professional career has been focused on enterprise IT systems administration, infrastructure management and supporting the technology platforms that organisations depend on.

Over the past two decades, I have worked extensively with Windows Server, Active Directory, networking, virtualisation, storage, automation and enterprise support environments within the public sector.

My approach to technology has always been centred around understanding systems as a whole — identifying how components interact, finding root causes and building practical solutions to complex technical problems.

In recent years, I have expanded into software engineering and AI-assisted development workflows, using modern tools to design, prototype and build increasingly ambitious applications and research projects.
