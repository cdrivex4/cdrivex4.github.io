About Me

I am the System Support Manager at the Seychelles Infrastructure Agency (SIA), where my professional career has been focused on enterprise IT systems administration, infrastructure management and supporting the technology platforms that organisations depend on.

Over the past two decades, I have worked extensively with Windows Server, Active Directory, networking, virtualisation, storage, automation and enterprise support environments within the public sector.

My approach to technology has always been centred around understanding systems as a whole — identifying how components interact, finding root causes and developing practical solutions to complex technical problems.

Much of my technical knowledge has been developed through hands-on experience, continuous learning and a determination to understand how things work.

In recent years, I have expanded my interests into software engineering and AI-assisted development workflows, using modern tools to help design, prototype and build increasingly ambitious applications and research projects.

This website documents that journey — showcasing my projects, technical experiments, research and ongoing exploration of modern technology.
